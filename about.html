About US Cable Internet

An independent comparison site for internet, TV and wireless plans.

US Cable Internet is an independent comparison site for internet, TV and wireless plans in the United States. We link directly to official provider pages. We may earn a commission when you order through links on this site.

Our mission

  • Rate transparency: clear starting prices and conditions.
  • Broadband equality: rural satellite options and urban fiber, side by side.
  • Easy filtering: by technology, price and speed.

Our methodology

We compare providers using published carrier plan pages and FCC broadband data. Prices shown are starting or promotional rates and can change; confirm on the provider site.

Disclosure: we may receive referral compensation from carriers. It does not decide which plans we list.

FCC broadband labels

Providers must show standardized broadband labels. They disclose the base monthly price, recurring fees such as equipment rental, typical speeds and latency, and data allowances.
