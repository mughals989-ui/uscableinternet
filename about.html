About US Cable Internet

US Cable Internet helps people in the United States compare internet, TV and wireless providers and find out who serves their address.

You can reach us by email at info@uscableinternet.com or by phone at 888-630-3881, Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM Eastern.

What we do

We organize provider and plan information by state and city, explain the differences between fiber, cable, 5G home internet and satellite, and send you to the provider's official site or to our team to check your exact address.

How we compare providers

We build our pages from the providers' own public plan pages, the FCC National Broadband Map and the FCC broadband labels that providers must publish. We describe coverage in general terms, such as which providers serve which parts of a state. Availability depends on your exact address, so we always tell you to confirm it with the provider.

We review our state pages regularly and show a "last reviewed" date on each one. We do not publish prices unless we can date them, because providers change prices and promotions often.

How we make money

This site is free to use. Some links are affiliate links, and we may earn a commission when you order through them. Calls to our number may be connected to a provider or an authorized sales partner. Commissions do not decide which providers we list or what we say about coverage. See our affiliate disclosure.

Corrections

If you see something wrong, such as a provider that does not serve a state or a plan that has changed, email info@uscableinternet.com. We check every report and fix errors quickly.
