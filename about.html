How we compare providers

We use provider-published plan information, coverage resources such as the FCC National Broadband Map, and provider broadband labels where available. We compare connection types and publicly described plans, and we aim to identify when an offer or price needs additional verification. Network footprints and speeds can differ within the same city, so only the provider can confirm service at a specific address.

How often is information reviewed?

We review pages and update them as information changes. Promotions, prices, equipment charges, speed ranges and package names can change without notice. Where a page shows a review date, that date refers to our most recent content review—not a guarantee that every provider offer remains unchanged.
