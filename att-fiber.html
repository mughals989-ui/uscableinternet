AT&T Fiber Plans: Speeds, Pricing & Availability

AT&T Fiber is available in parts of the United States. Fiber plans can offer symmetrical upload and download speeds, but the available tiers and offers depend on the exact service address.

Who may want to compare AT&T Fiber?

Households that upload large files, work remotely, make frequent video calls or want low-latency wired internet—if AT&T Fiber reaches their address.

Check your addressVisit AT&T Fiber official site

What to verify before ordering

  1. Verify that the address qualifies for AT&T Fiber, not just another AT&T internet technology.
  2. Compare download and upload speeds at the same tier; fiber tiers may offer symmetrical speeds.
  3. Check the advertised price conditions, including AutoPay and paperless billing requirements.
  4. Confirm whether equipment is included and whether installation or other one-time fees apply.
  5. Review the broadband facts label, price guarantee or promotional terms, and the final checkout amount.

Compare it with other home internet options

Do not assume the provider serves every address in a city or ZIP code. Compare the actual offers available at your home with other technologies, including fiber internet alternatives where available.
