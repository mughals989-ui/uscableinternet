AT&T Fiber Plans: Speeds, Pricing & Availability

AT&T Fiber is available in parts of the United States. Fiber plans can offer symmetrical upload and download speeds, but the available tiers and offers depend on the exact service address.

Who may want to compare AT&T Fiber?

Households that upload large files, work remotely, make frequent video calls or want low-latency wired internet—if AT&T Fiber reaches their address.

What to verify before ordering

  1. Verify that the address qualifies for AT&T Fiber, not just another AT&T internet technology.
  2. Compare download and upload speeds at the same tier; fiber tiers may offer symmetrical speeds.
  3. Check the advertised price conditions, including AutoPay and paperless billing requirements.
  4. Confirm whether equipment is included and whether installation or other one-time fees apply.
  5. Review the broadband facts label, price guarantee or promotional terms, and the final checkout amount.

AT&T Fiber: confirm fiber, not just AT&T availability

AT&T sells more than one type of home internet service. An address that can get AT&T Internet is not automatically eligible for AT&T Fiber. Confirm that the checkout page specifically identifies Fiber and compare the upload and download speeds for that fiber tier.

The advertised price may depend on eligible AutoPay and paperless billing. Check the regular monthly rate, equipment terms, installation, taxes and any one-time charges. If symmetrical speeds are important for remote work, video calls or cloud backups, verify the exact upload speed in the offer details.

Common questions

How can I tell whether an address has AT&T Fiber?

Enter the complete address in AT&T's availability checker and confirm that the result explicitly says Fiber and shows a fiber speed tier.

Is AT&T Fiber available statewide?

No. Fiber buildout is limited to selected neighborhoods and addresses. Nearby homes may have different service options.

Compare the actual offer at your home with other internet plans, review fiber options and check availability at your address before choosing.

Frequently asked questions about AT&T Fiber

How can I tell whether an AT&T plan is actually fiber?

Check the exact plan technology and broadband facts label. AT&T offers different home internet products; availability of AT&T service does not automatically mean the address qualifies for AT&T Fiber.

Is AT&T Fiber available throughout a city?

No. Fiber availability depends on where the network has been built and can change from one street or building to another. Use AT&T's official address checker to confirm.

Are AT&T Fiber download and upload speeds symmetrical?

Many AT&T Fiber tiers advertise matching download and upload speeds, but the available tier depends on the address and plan. Confirm the exact speed details in the current offer and broadband facts label.

What conditions can affect the advertised AT&T price?

Check AutoPay and paperless billing requirements, promotional eligibility, equipment, installation, taxes and the regular rate. Compare the full monthly cost rather than only the headline offer.
