Frequently asked questions

Do channel numbers vary by location?

Yes. Channel numbers and exact lineups vary by market and ZIP code. Always confirm on the provider’s official website for your address.

Which package has the most channels?

Higher tiers such as Spectrum Stream TV VIP, Xfinity TV Premium, DIRECTV Premier and DISH America’s Top 250 generally include the largest lineups. Premium movie channels are often add-ons.

Are sports channels included in base packages?

ESPN and ESPN2 are commonly included in mid-tier and higher packages. Regional sports networks and specialty sports channels often require higher tiers or add-ons.
