Check Internet Providers At Your Address

Enter a full U.S. address or ZIP code to see internet providers reported in that area.

We do not store your full address on our servers. Address details are sent to a third-party geocoding service to locate the area; coordinates are then used to request area-level provider information. Results are not a guarantee of exact-address service.

How this checker works

Enter a full street address or ZIP code in the same search box. A street address helps locate a more precise area; a ZIP code provides a broader search. We use a third-party geocoding service to locate the area, then request area-level provider data where available. This is a shortlist—not a live confirmation that a provider serves your exact home.

Before ordering, confirm availability, speed, monthly price, equipment fees and promotional terms on the provider’s official website. You can also compare internet plans, fiber internet, 5G home internet, or browse providers by state.

Frequently asked questions

What address details do I need?

Enter a full street address with city and state, or use a 5-digit ZIP code.

Do you save my address?

No. It is used in your browser to show providers and is not sent to us.

Does this confirm service at my address?

No. It lists providers to check. Confirm availability on each provider's official site.
