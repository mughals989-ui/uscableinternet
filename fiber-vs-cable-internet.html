Fiber vs Cable Internet: Speed, Cost and Availability

Fiber typically offers faster uploads and lower latency. Cable is more widely available in many areas and can still deliver high download speeds. The best choice depends on what is available at your address.

Quick comparison

  • Uploads: Fiber is often symmetrical; cable uploads are usually lower
  • Latency: Fiber is generally lower
  • Availability: Cable covers more addresses in many markets
  • Price: Depends on promos, equipment and contract terms
