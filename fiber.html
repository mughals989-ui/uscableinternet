Frequently asked questions

What is fiber internet?

Fiber internet sends data as light through glass cables. It offers high speeds, low latency and equal upload and download speeds.

Which fiber plans are listed here?

Verizon Fios from $30 per month with a mobile plan (300-940 Mbps) and AT&T Internet and Fiber from $35 per month (300-1,000 Mbps).

Is fiber available everywhere?

No. Fiber is mostly in metro and suburban areas. Enter your full address to see what to check for your home.
