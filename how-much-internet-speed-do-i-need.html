How Much Internet Speed Do I Need at Home?

The right speed depends on how many people use the connection at once and what they do—streaming, video calls, gaming or large uploads.

  • ~100 Mbps: Light use, 1–2 people, HD streaming
  • ~300 Mbps: Families, multiple streams, video calls
  • 1 Gbps+: Heavy use, 4K, large uploads, many devices
