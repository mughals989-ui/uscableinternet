FCC Broadband Label Guide

Providers must show standardized broadband labels. They disclose:

  • Base monthly price
  • Recurring fees such as equipment rental
  • Typical speeds and latency
  • Data allowances

Internet & TV Plan FAQs

How do I find internet providers at my address?

Enter your street address or ZIP code in the checker at the top of the page, then confirm availability on each provider's official site.

What is the cheapest home internet plan?

Starting prices begin around $30 per month for Spectrum and Verizon Fios, and $35 for AT&T and T-Mobile 5G Home. Prices vary by address.

Is fiber better than cable or satellite?

Fiber offers the fastest speeds, equal upload speeds and the lowest latency. Satellite is for areas without wired service.

Which TV option has no cable box fees?

Spectrum TV Select Plus lists no cable box fees. DIRECTV Stream includes unlimited cloud DVR and DISH Top 120 offers a 2-year price guarantee.

Does US Cable Internet charge for comparisons?

No. The site is free and may earn a commission from some providers.
