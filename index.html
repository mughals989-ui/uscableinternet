Frequently asked questions

How do I check internet providers at my full address?

Enter your street address, city, state and ZIP code in the checker, then confirm availability on each provider's official site. A full address gives a more accurate result than a ZIP code.

Why does my full address matter and not just my ZIP code?

Providers serve specific streets and buildings, so two homes in the same ZIP code can have different options. The exact address decides what is available.

What is the cheapest home internet plan?

Starting prices begin around $30 per month for Spectrum and Verizon Fios, and $35 for AT&T and T-Mobile 5G Home. Prices vary by address.

Does US Cable Internet charge for comparisons?

No. The site is free and may earn a commission from some providers.
