Broadband Research

  1. 5G home internet is the fastest-growing home broadband segment in suburban and exurban markets.

  3. Low-orbit satellite has cut rural latency sharply.

FCC Broadband Label Guide

Providers must show standardized broadband labels. They disclose:

  • Base monthly price
  • Recurring fees such as equipment rental
  • Typical speeds and latency
  • Data allowances
