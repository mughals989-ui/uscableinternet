Choose the right connection type

Each technology has different strengths. Availability is the deciding factor at most addresses.

Fiber

Fastest uploads and lowest latency. Ideal for remote work, gaming and multi-device homes. Limited availability.

Cable

Widely available high-speed option. Strong downloads; uploads usually lower than fiber. Good all-around choice.

5G Home Internet

Wireless fixed broadband. Easy self-install. Speeds depend on signal strength and network congestion.

Satellite

Available nearly nationwide. Best when no wired or 5G options exist. Higher latency and data considerations.

Frequently asked questions

How do I find the best internet provider for my address?

Use the address bar above or go to the Check Address page. Availability, speeds and prices are location-specific. Always confirm the final offer directly with the provider.

What is the cheapest home internet right now?

Promotional starting prices often fall in the $30–$50 range from Spectrum, Xfinity, AT&T Fiber and T-Mobile 5G Home. These rates usually require AutoPay or paperless billing and may increase after the promo period.

Is fiber better than cable?

Fiber generally offers faster uploads and more consistent performance. Cable is more widely available and can still deliver excellent download speeds.

Does 5G home internet work well?

It can be a strong option where the signal is good. Speeds typically range from about 70–250 Mbps and can vary with network load. Most providers offer a trial period.
