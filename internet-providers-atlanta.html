Internet providers to compare in Atlanta

Options worth checking include AT&T Fiber, Google Fiber, Xfinity and T-Mobile Home Internet. This is not a guarantee that each provider serves every address.

  • Fiber internet: A strong option for fast uploads, video calls and busy households when service is available.
  • Cable internet: Often worth comparing for streaming, gaming and multiple connected devices.
  • 5G home internet: Availability and performance depend on provider eligibility, local network capacity and indoor signal.
  • Satellite internet: May be worth checking for addresses outside reliable wired or cellular coverage; review equipment, latency and monthly costs.

How to choose a plan in Atlanta

Compare wired fiber and cable plans first, then check 5G home internet as an alternative where service is offered.

Service can differ between nearby streets and apartment buildings. Fiber availability may depend on the building, while cable and 5G eligibility also need address-level confirmation.

Compare the regular monthly rate as well as any introductory price, equipment charges, data policies, installation fees and contract terms. For most households, the right plan is the one that meets your needs at a fair total cost—not simply the one with the highest advertised speed.
