Neighborhood note

Downtown, Homewood, Vestavia, Hoover and other suburbs do not share one provider list. Apartment buildings may be limited to a bulk provider. Always check the exact service address—including unit number when applicable.

FAQ

Who provides internet in Birmingham?

Spectrum, AT&T Fiber (where built), Xfinity in some areas, 5G home from T-Mobile or Verizon, and satellite options. Confirm at your address.

Is fiber available in Birmingham?

In parts of the metro. AT&T Fiber is the primary widely marketed fiber brand; coverage is not universal.
