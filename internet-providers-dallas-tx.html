Internet Providers in Dallas, TX

Dallas has strong fiber coverage from AT&T, solid cable from Spectrum and Xfinity, and 5G home internet to fill in the gaps. Which provider you can get depends on whether you live in a Downtown high-rise, an Oak Cliff bungalow or a new home in the northern suburbs. Here is how the options compare.

Last reviewed: October 2026. Plans, prices and availability change often. Confirm with the provider for your address.

Top internet providers in Dallas

Best fiber

AT&T Fiber

AT&T's fiber network is the most widespread in the Dallas area. Symmetrical speeds make it well suited to remote work, video calls and large uploads.

  • Plans from 300 Mbps up to multi-gig
  • No data caps on fiber plans
  • Coverage varies block by block

Main cable option

Spectrum

Spectrum serves much of the city of Dallas with fast cable downloads, many speed tiers and a modem included on most plans.

  • Plans from about 500 Mbps to multi-gig in select areas
  • Good where fiber has not arrived
  • Uploads are slower than fiber

Parts of the metro

Xfinity

Xfinity covers parts of the wider Dallas-Fort Worth area. If it serves your address, it is worth comparing against Spectrum and AT&T Fiber.

  • Plans from about 150 Mbps up to multi-gig in select areas
  • Mobile and TV bundles available
  • Often runs new-customer promotions

Frontier

Internet by area of Dallas

Downtown, Uptown and Deep Ellum

High-rise apartments and lofts mean the building often decides what is wired in. Ask management which providers are installed before you order.

Oak Lawn and Turtle Creek

A mix of condos and older buildings. Fiber and cable overlap in many spots, so compare current offers for your exact address.

East Dallas and Lakewood

Older homes with a mix of fiber and cable. Check AT&T Fiber and Spectrum side by side, since coverage can differ by street.

Oak Cliff and South Dallas

Cable is widely available, with fiber reaching many neighborhoods. Discounted plans for eligible households are worth checking here.

Plano, Frisco and McKinney

Newer suburban neighborhoods often have both fiber and cable. Some communities have builder or HOA arrangements, so ask what yours includes.

Staying connected in Dallas weather

North Texas sees hail, tornado season storms and occasional winter freezes that can cause outages. If you work from home, keep a 5G home plan or phone hotspot as a backup, and put your modem and router on a battery backup so brief power losses do not drop your connection.

Low-income internet plans in Dallas

The federal Affordable Connectivity Program has ended, but providers still run discounted plans. Look at AT&T Access, Spectrum Internet Assist and Xfinity Internet Essentials, depending on who serves your address. Qualification often depends on programs like SNAP, Medicaid or SSI. Prices and rules change, so confirm on the provider's site.

How to choose the right Dallas plan

Check your exact address. Providers can differ from one block to the next, so a ZIP code is not enough.

Match speed to your household. 300 to 500 Mbps works for most homes. Several remote workers, gamers or heavy streamers should consider 1 Gig.

Prefer fiber if uploads matter. Matching upload speeds help with video meetings and large file transfers.

Read the price details. Compare the promo price, how long it lasts, equipment fees and the regular rate afterward.

Renting? Ask your landlord what is already wired. A 5G home plan skips the technician visit.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best internet provider in Dallas?

AT&T Fiber is usually the best choice where it is available thanks to symmetrical speeds. Spectrum is the main cable option across much of the city.

Is Spectrum or Xfinity available in Dallas?

Spectrum serves much of the city of Dallas, while Xfinity serves parts of the wider metro. Which one you can get depends on your exact address.

Is fiber internet available in the Dallas area?

Yes. AT&T Fiber covers many Dallas neighborhoods and suburbs, and Frontier Fiber reaches select areas. Check your exact address.

Are there low-income internet plans in Dallas?

Yes. AT&T Access, Spectrum Internet Assist and Xfinity Internet Essentials offer discounted plans to qualifying households. Eligibility rules apply.
