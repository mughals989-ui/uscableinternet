Compare internet options across Florida

Florida households may be able to choose from fiber, cable, 5G home internet, fixed wireless or satellite, but options can differ by neighborhood and even by apartment building. Fiber is worth comparing for symmetrical uploads, while cable may offer fast downloads in established service areas. 5G can be a convenient alternative when signal quality and provider eligibility fit your address.

Before signing up, compare the regular monthly price after promotions, equipment costs, upload speeds, data policies and cancellation terms. In condos and apartments, ask building management which providers can serve the unit, then verify directly with the provider.

Explore our guides to fiber internet, home internet plans and 5G home internet, or use the address checker to continue comparing options. Always confirm final availability and plan terms with the provider.

Rural Florida internet

Inland and Panhandle counties often rely on 5G home internet, fixed wireless or satellite. Check each provider's address tool before you commit.

Hurricane season

Hurricanes can cause multi-day outages for every provider. Keep a battery backup for the modem and router, and a mobile hotspot as a backup connection.

Low-income internet plans in Florida

The federal Affordable Connectivity Program has ended, but providers still offer discounted plans. Look at AT&T Access, Xfinity Internet Essentials and Spectrum Internet Assist, plus the federal Lifeline program. Eligibility often depends on programs like SNAP, Medicaid or SSI. Confirm current rules on the provider's website.

How to choose the right plan in Florida

Check your exact address. Coverage changes from street to street, even in one city.

Pick speed for your household. 300 to 500 Mbps suits most homes. Several remote workers or heavy gaming and streaming may need 1 Gig.

Prefer fiber when you can get it. It is the most consistent option for uploads and video calls.

Read the price details. Compare the promo price, how long it lasts, equipment fees and the regular rate after.

Frequently asked questions

Which internet is best in South Florida?

Xfinity cable and AT&T Fiber are the common choices. Fiber is better for uploads where it is available at your address.

Can I get internet in a condo or apartment?

It depends on the building. Many buildings have exclusive or preferred provider agreements, so ask management first.
