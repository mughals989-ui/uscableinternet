Rural Florida / Hurricane season

Inland and Panhandle counties often rely on 5G home, fixed wireless or satellite. Hurricanes can cause multi-day outages—keep battery backup and a mobile hotspot.

FAQ

Best internet in South Florida?

Xfinity cable and AT&T Fiber are common. Fiber is better for uploads where available.

Condo or apartment internet?

Many buildings have preferred or exclusive providers—ask management first.
