Rural Georgia internet

South Georgia and rural counties often rely on fixed wireless, 5G home or satellite.

Storm and hurricane season

Severe storms and coastal hurricanes can cause outages. Battery backup and a hotspot help.

Low-income internet plans in Georgia

The federal Affordable Connectivity Program has ended. Look at AT&T Access, Xfinity Internet Essentials and federal Lifeline.
