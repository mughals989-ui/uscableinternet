Internet options across Indiana

Indiana has a mix of urban markets, university communities, suburbs, industrial cities and rural areas. Cable and fiber availability is highly localized; neighboring addresses may have different choices. In smaller towns and rural areas, fixed wireless or satellite may be worth comparing when wired service is limited.

Fiber internet

Compare fiber where available for strong upload performance and lower latency. Confirm the exact address.

Cable internet

Check speed tiers, upload speeds, equipment charges and the regular rate after introductory pricing.

5G home internet

Check eligibility and network capacity at the home; actual speeds vary with signal and congestion.

Satellite internet

Compare equipment, installation, data policies, latency and total monthly cost in remote areas.

Local areas to compare

Indianapolis: Parts of the metro have fiber from providers such as AT&T Fiber and Metronet, and cable from providers such as Xfinity. Coverage is still neighborhood-specific—confirm the full street address before you order.

Fort Wayne, South Bend, Evansville, Bloomington, Lafayette and other cities: Wired options can differ by development. Compare cable and fiber first when they appear for the address.

Rural Indiana: Farm and small-town addresses often have fewer wired choices. Fixed wireless, 5G home internet, regional fiber builders, and satellite may be relevant. Verify each option at the exact address.

How to compare plans

Enter the complete service address on each provider's official website. Compare the broadband facts label, typical upload and download speeds, latency, installation, equipment, taxes, data limits, AutoPay conditions and the regular monthly rate after promotions.

Indiana internet FAQs

Who offers fiber in parts of Indianapolis?

AT&T Fiber and Metronet cover parts of the Indianapolis area. Coverage is not citywide—confirm the exact address with each provider.

Is fiber internet available in Indiana?

Fiber is available in selected communities, but coverage is highly local. Check AT&T, Frontier, Metronet and other local operators at the exact address.

What internet options are available in rural Indiana?

Rural options may include regional wired networks, fixed wireless and satellite. Compare installation terms and total cost at the exact address.

What to verify before choosing an internet plan in Indiana

Provider names on a state guide are a starting point, not confirmation that service reaches every street, apartment or rural address in Indiana. Compare plans using the complete service address and the provider's current offer.

  • Confirm the exact address. Check the house number, apartment or unit, and ZIP code with the provider; nearby homes can have different networks.
  • Compare the real monthly cost. Check how long the promotional rate lasts, the regular rate afterward, equipment rental, installation, taxes, fees and any AutoPay or bundle requirements.
  • Compare performance details. Review upload as well as download speed, data limits, latency where relevant, contract terms and cancellation conditions.
  • Check building access. Renters and condo residents should ask whether the building is wired for the provider and whether installation needs approval.
