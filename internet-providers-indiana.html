Internet options across Indiana

Indiana has a mix of urban markets, university communities, suburbs, industrial cities and rural areas. Cable and fiber availability is highly localized; neighboring addresses may have different choices.

  • Fiber — Compare where available for strong uploads and lower latency; confirm the exact address.
  • Cable — Check speed tiers, equipment charges and the regular rate after introductory pricing.
  • 5G home — Check eligibility and network capacity at the home.
  • Satellite — Compare equipment, installation, data policies and latency in remote areas.

Local areas to compare

Indianapolis: Parts of the metro have fiber from providers such as AT&T Fiber and Metronet, and cable from providers such as Xfinity. Coverage is still neighborhood-specific—confirm the full street address before you order.

Fort Wayne, South Bend, Evansville, Bloomington, Lafayette and other cities: Wired options can differ by development. Compare cable and fiber first when they appear for the address.

Rural Indiana: Farm and small-town addresses often have fewer wired choices. Fixed wireless, 5G home internet, regional fiber builders and satellite may be relevant. Verify each option at the exact address.

How to compare plans

Enter the complete service address on each provider’s official website. Compare the broadband facts label, typical speeds, installation, equipment, taxes, data limits, AutoPay conditions and the regular monthly rate after promotions.

Indiana internet FAQs

Who offers fiber in parts of Indianapolis?

AT&T Fiber and Metronet cover parts of the Indianapolis area. Coverage is not citywide—confirm the exact address with each provider.

Is fiber internet available in Indiana?

Fiber is available in selected communities, but coverage is highly local. Check AT&T, Frontier, Metronet and other local operators at the exact address.

What internet options are available in rural Indiana?

Rural options may include regional wired networks, fixed wireless and satellite. Compare installation terms and total cost at the exact address.

How do I compare internet plans in Indiana?

Confirm the exact address, then compare promotional term, regular monthly rate, equipment, installation, data limits and contract terms with each provider.

What to verify before choosing an internet plan in Indiana

Provider names on a state guide are a starting point, not confirmation that service reaches every street, apartment or rural address in Indiana.

  • Confirm the exact address — house number, unit and ZIP; nearby homes can have different networks.
  • Compare the real monthly cost — promo length, regular rate, equipment, installation, taxes, fees and AutoPay or bundle rules.
  • Compare performance details — upload and download, data limits, latency where relevant, contract and cancellation terms.
  • Check building access — renters and condo residents should ask about wiring and installation approval.
