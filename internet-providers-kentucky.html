Rural Kentucky internet

Eastern Kentucky and many rural counties have limited wired options. Fixed wireless, 5G home internet and Starlink are the main alternatives.

How to choose the right plan in Kentucky

Check your exact address. Coverage changes from street to street.

Prefer fiber when available. Better for uploads and consistency.

Match speed to your household. 300–500 Mbps is enough for most homes.
