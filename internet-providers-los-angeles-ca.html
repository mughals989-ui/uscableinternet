Internet Providers in Los Angeles, CA

Los Angeles is spread across hundreds of square miles, and the internet options change from one neighborhood to the next. A fiber-ready block in Silver Lake can sit next to a street that only has cable. This guide shows who serves which part of the city and how to pick the right plan.

Last reviewed: October 2026. Plans, prices and availability change often. Confirm with the provider for your address.

Internet by area of Los Angeles

San Fernando Valley

Larger houses and wider lots mean many single-family homes can pick between Spectrum and AT&T Fiber. Fiber coverage is strong in many Valley neighborhoods.

Westside and Beach Cities

Santa Monica, Venice, West LA and nearby areas tend to have the most competition, with fiber and cable overlapping often.

Downtown and Hollywood

Apartment towers and lofts are common. Your building usually decides what is wired in, so ask management which providers are already installed.

Eastside and Northeast LA

Silver Lake, Echo Park, Highland Park and Eagle Rock have a mix of fiber and cable. Older housing stock can affect installation, so check before you book.

South LA and Harbor

Spectrum is widely available, with AT&T Fiber expanding in many areas. Discounted plans for eligible households are worth checking here.

Low-income internet plans in Los Angeles

The federal Affordable Connectivity Program has ended, but providers still offer their own discounted plans. In LA, look at AT&T Access and Spectrum Internet Assist. Qualification often depends on programs like SNAP, SSI, Medi-Cal or free school lunch. Prices and rules change, so confirm on the provider's site.

How to choose the right LA plan

Check your exact address first. In Los Angeles, coverage can change from one block to the next, so a ZIP code is not enough.

Pick speed for how you use it. Streaming and browsing for one or two people works well at 300 to 500 Mbps. A household with remote workers, gamers or heavy cloud use should look at 1 Gig fiber.

Prefer fiber if uploads matter. Fiber's matching upload speeds help with video meetings and large file transfers.

Watch the price after the promo. Compare the introductory price, how long it lasts, equipment fees and the regular rate afterward.

Renting in an apartment? Ask your landlord what is already wired. If nothing suits you, a 5G home plan avoids a technician visit.

Frequently asked questions

Who is the best internet provider in Los Angeles?

AT&T Fiber is usually the best choice where it is available because of its symmetrical speeds. Spectrum is the most widely available option across the city.

Is AT&T Fiber available everywhere in LA?

No. AT&T Fiber covers many neighborhoods but not every street. Check your exact address, since availability can differ between neighboring blocks.

What internet speed do I need in Los Angeles?

Most households do well with 300 to 500 Mbps. Homes with several remote workers, creators or gamers should consider 1 Gig fiber.

Are there low-income internet plans in Los Angeles?

Yes. AT&T Access and Spectrum Internet Assist offer discounted plans to qualifying households. Eligibility rules apply.
