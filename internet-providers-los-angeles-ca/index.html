Internet Providers in Los Angeles, CA

Los Angeles covers a large area with apartments, hillside homes, older buildings and suburban neighborhoods. Internet service can vary between nearby streets and even between units in the same building. AT&T Fiber, Spectrum and Frontier are wired options to investigate, while T-Mobile and Verizon offer wireless home internet where eligible.

Internet by area of Los Angeles

San Fernando Valley

The Valley includes detached homes, apartments and a variety of residential developments. Check wired and wireless options separately rather than assuming a provider serving one neighborhood also serves another. For a house, ask whether the existing connection point can be reused.

Westside and Beach Communities

Santa Monica, Venice and West Los Angeles include apartments, condos and single-family homes. Building wiring and property access can affect installation. Residents in multi-unit buildings should check with management before scheduling a technician.

Downtown and Hollywood

High-rise apartments, condos and converted buildings can have existing provider arrangements or restrictions on new wiring. Ask which providers are already installed, whether the building permits additional installations and whether service is available in your individual unit.

Eastside and Northeast Los Angeles

Silver Lake, Echo Park, Highland Park and Eagle Rock include older properties and hillside homes. Property layout and existing wiring may affect installation. Confirm the installation process with the provider before placing an order.

South Los Angeles and Harbor Areas

These areas include apartments, detached homes and other residential properties. Provider availability can vary across nearby streets. Check your complete address with each provider you are considering.

Staying connected during wildfire weather and power outages

Los Angeles County prepares residents for hazards that include wildfires, earthquakes, heavy rain, flooding and power outages. Power interruptions can disrupt home internet even when a provider's network is otherwise operating. Keep phones and backup batteries charged, know how to receive local emergency alerts and consider a battery backup for your modem and router. During an evacuation warning or order, follow instructions from emergency officials.

For local preparedness information, visit Ready LA County, the county's official emergency preparedness resource.

Frequently asked questions

What internet providers can I check in Los Angeles?

You can check AT&T Fiber, Spectrum, Frontier Fiber, T-Mobile Home Internet and Verizon 5G Home. Availability and connection types depend on your address.

No. AT&T says Fiber is available in parts of Los Angeles. Use its official address checker to see whether service is offered at your home.

Does Frontier offer fiber internet in Los Angeles?
