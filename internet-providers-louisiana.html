Rural Louisiana internet

Many rural parishes rely on fixed wireless, 5G home internet or satellite. Local providers and cooperatives also serve some communities.

How to choose the right plan in Louisiana

Check your exact address. Coverage changes from street to street.

Prefer fiber when available. Better for uploads and consistency.

Match speed to your household. 300–500 Mbps is enough for most homes.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best internet in New Orleans?

Cox is a major cable provider; AT&T Fiber is available in some neighborhoods.

Is fiber available in Baton Rouge?

In parts of the city. Check AT&T and other providers for your address.

What works in rural Louisiana?

5G home internet, fixed wireless and Starlink are the usual options.
