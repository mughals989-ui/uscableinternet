Rural Louisiana internet

Rural parishes often have limited wired service. Check local carriers, fixed wireless and 5G home, then satellite.

Hurricane season and flooding

Hurricanes and flooding can cause multi-day outages. A battery backup for the modem and router and a phone hotspot keep you connected during short outages.

Low-income internet plans in Louisiana

The federal Affordable Connectivity Program has ended, but providers still offer discounted plans. Look at Cox ConnectAssist, Optimum Advantage Internet and the federal Lifeline program. Eligibility often depends on programs like SNAP, Medicaid or SSI. Confirm current rules on the provider's website.

How to choose the right plan in Louisiana

Check your exact address. Coverage changes from street to street, even in one city.

Pick speed for your household. 300 to 500 Mbps suits most homes. Several remote workers or heavy gaming and streaming may need 1 Gig.

Prefer fiber when you can get it. It is the most consistent option for uploads and video calls.

Read the price details. Compare the promo price, how long it lasts, equipment fees and the regular rate after.
