How to choose internet in Maine

  1. Check your address — Coverage differs street by street.
  2. Compare connection types — Fiber (where built), cable, 5G home, and satellite each trade speed, latency, and price.
  3. Read the full price — Promo length, equipment fees, and the regular rate after the intro period matter.

What to verify before choosing an internet plan in Maine

Provider names on a state guide are a starting point, not confirmation that service reaches every street, apartment or rural address in Maine. Compare plans using the complete service address and the provider's current offer.

  • Confirm the exact address. Check the house number, apartment or unit, and ZIP code with the provider; nearby homes can have different networks.
  • Compare the real monthly cost. Check how long the promotional rate lasts, the regular rate afterward, equipment rental, installation, taxes, fees and any AutoPay or bundle requirements.
  • Compare performance details. Review upload as well as download speed, data limits, latency where relevant, contract terms and cancellation conditions.
  • Check building access. Renters and condo residents should ask whether the building is wired for the provider and whether installation needs approval.

The FCC National Broadband Map is useful for researching reported coverage, but it is not an order confirmation. Frequently asked questions

What is the best internet in Maine?

Cable (Spectrum or Xfinity) where available; 5G home or Starlink in many rural areas. Always verify at your exact address.

Is fiber available in Portland, ME?

Fiber is limited in Maine compared with some states. Check AT&T and local fiber builds for your street.

What works in rural Maine?

5G home internet where signal is strong, fixed wireless, and satellite (including Starlink).
