Compare internet options across Maine

Internet availability depends on the exact service address, not just a city or ZIP code. Compare wired service in larger towns with fixed wireless or satellite options in more remote and coastal areas. Where available, compare fiber and cable plans first; 5G home internet or fixed wireless may be alternatives at some addresses, while satellite can help in more remote locations.

Rural Maine internet

Large parts of Maine have limited wired broadband. Fixed wireless, 5G home internet and Starlink are the most common solutions outside the main population centers.

How to choose the right plan in Maine

Check your exact address. Coverage changes from street to street.

Prefer fiber when available. Better for uploads and consistency.

Match speed to your household. 300–500 Mbps is enough for most homes.
