What to verify before choosing an internet plan in Maryland

Provider names on a state guide are a starting point, not confirmation that service reaches every street, apartment or rural address in Maryland. Compare plans using the complete service address and the provider's current offer.

  • Confirm the exact address. Check the house number, apartment or unit, and ZIP code with the provider; nearby homes can have different networks.
  • Compare the real monthly cost. Check how long the promotional rate lasts, the regular rate afterward, equipment rental, installation, taxes, fees and any AutoPay or bundle requirements.
  • Compare performance details. Review upload as well as download speed, data limits, latency where relevant, contract terms and cancellation conditions.
  • Check building access. Renters and condo residents should ask whether the building is wired for the provider and whether installation needs approval.

The FCC National Broadband Map is useful for researching reported coverage, but it is not an order confirmation. Internet availability across Maryland

Compare options in communities such as Baltimore, Columbia, Annapolis, Frederick, Hagerstown and the Eastern Shore. The Baltimore–Washington corridor has dense neighborhoods and apartment buildings, while western Maryland's mountains and the Eastern Shore have different network footprints. In condos and rentals, verify service for the exact building and unit; a provider serving the surrounding ZIP code may not have wiring in your property.

Which connection type should you compare?

Start with fiber and cable if they are offered at the address, then compare 5G home internet or fixed wireless where signal and eligibility are confirmed. Satellite can be a practical alternative for remote homes, but review latency, data policies, equipment costs and weather considerations. Do not assume a technology is available just because it appears in a statewide provider list.

Questions to ask before ordering

  • Is the exact street address and apartment or unit eligible for a new installation?
  • What is the monthly price after the introductory period, and are equipment, installation or other fees extra?
  • Are upload speeds, data limits, contract terms and cancellation fees acceptable for the household?
  • Can the provider confirm the advertised speed tier at this specific address?
