Rural Maryland internet

Western Maryland mountains and Eastern Shore towns may have one wired provider. Check fixed wireless and 5G home, then satellite.

Hurricanes and winter storms

Coastal storms and winter weather can interrupt service. A battery backup for the modem and router and a phone hotspot keep you connected during short outages.

Low-income internet plans in Maryland

The federal Affordable Connectivity Program has ended, but providers still offer discounted plans. Look at Xfinity Internet Essentials, Verizon Fios Forward and the federal Lifeline program. Eligibility often depends on programs like SNAP, Medicaid or SSI. Confirm current rules on the provider's website.

How to choose the right plan in Maryland

Check your exact address. Coverage changes from street to street, even in one city.

Pick speed for your household. 300 to 500 Mbps suits most homes. Several remote workers or heavy gaming and streaming may need 1 Gig.

Prefer fiber when you can get it. It is the most consistent option for uploads and video calls.

Read the price details. Compare the promo price, how long it lasts, equipment fees and the regular rate after.
