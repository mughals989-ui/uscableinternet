Rural Mississippi internet

Many rural counties rely on fixed wireless, 5G home internet or satellite. Local providers and cooperatives also serve some communities.

How to choose the right plan in Mississippi

Check your exact address. Coverage changes from street to street.

Prefer fiber when available. Better for uploads and consistency.

Match speed to your household. 300–500 Mbps is enough for most homes.
