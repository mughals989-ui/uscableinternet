What to verify before choosing an internet plan in Missouri

Provider names on a state guide are a starting point, not confirmation that service reaches every street, apartment or rural address in Missouri. Compare plans using the complete service address and the provider's current offer.

  • Confirm the exact address. Check the house number, apartment or unit, and ZIP code with the provider; nearby homes can have different networks.
  • Compare the real monthly cost. Check how long the promotional rate lasts, the regular rate afterward, equipment rental, installation, taxes, fees and any AutoPay or bundle requirements.
  • Compare performance details. Review upload as well as download speed, data limits, latency where relevant, contract terms and cancellation conditions.
  • Check building access. Renters and condo residents should ask whether the building is wired for the provider and whether installation needs approval.

The FCC National Broadband Map is useful for researching reported coverage, but it is not an order confirmation. Internet availability across Missouri

Compare options in communities such as St. Louis, Kansas City, Springfield, Columbia, Jefferson City and Ozarks communities. St. Louis and Kansas City have dense metro networks, while the Ozarks, central Missouri and smaller northern communities have different coverage patterns. Hills, distance and housing density can affect service choices. For apartments, ask whether the building has provider wiring and whether installation is available to your unit.

Compare connection types at your address

Where fiber and cable are available, compare both rather than choosing from download speed alone. Check upload speed, latency, data limits and the standard monthly rate. If neither wired option reaches the property, compare 5G home internet or fixed wireless using the provider's eligibility check and expected signal at the home. Satellite may extend coverage to remote locations, but equipment costs, latency and data policies matter for video calls and gaming.

Before you choose a plan

  • Confirm the complete street address, including unit number, with every provider.
  • Ask when promotional pricing ends and what the regular price will be.
  • Check equipment rental, installation, taxes, surcharges and any AutoPay conditions.
  • Review upload speeds, data caps, contract length and cancellation rules.
