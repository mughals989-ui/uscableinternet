Rural Missouri internet

Rural Missouri often relies on fixed wireless, 5G home internet or satellite. Local telephone companies are expanding fiber in some counties.

How to choose the right plan in Missouri

Check your exact address. Coverage changes from street to street.

Prefer fiber when available. Better for uploads and consistency.

Match speed to your household. 300–500 Mbps is enough for most homes.
