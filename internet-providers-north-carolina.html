How to choose internet in North Carolina

  • Fiber for the fastest speeds and equal upload speeds, where it is offered.
  • Cable for wide availability and low starting prices.
  • 5G home for no-wire setup and a trial period.
  • Satellite when no wired service reaches your address.

Frequently asked questions

How do I find internet providers in North Carolina?

Enter your full street address, city, state and ZIP code in the checker, then confirm availability on each provider's official site. Providers serve specific streets, so your exact address decides what is available in North Carolina.

Is fiber available everywhere in North Carolina?

No. Fiber is mostly in metro and suburban areas, and it depends on your street. Enter your full address to see which providers to check.

What if I live in a rural part of North Carolina?

Satellite internet can help where no wired service reaches. HughesNet lists availability coast to coast and Starlink lists 50-220 Mbps. Confirm at your address.

What is the cheapest internet plan in North Carolina?

Starting prices in our comparison begin around $30 per month, and $35 for T-Mobile 5G Home with a voice line. Prices and availability vary by address in North Carolina.
