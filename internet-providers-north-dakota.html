Rural North Dakota internet

Rural North Dakota often depends on fixed wireless, 5G home internet or Starlink. Local cooperatives also provide service in some areas.

How to choose the right plan in North Dakota

Check your exact address. Coverage changes from street to street.

Prefer fiber when available. Better for uploads and consistency.

Match speed to your household. 300–500 Mbps is enough for most homes.
