Compare internet options across Rhode Island

Internet availability depends on the exact service address, not just a city or ZIP code. Compare providers by town and building, as network availability can differ across nearby neighborhoods. How to choose the right plan in Rhode Island

Check your exact address. Coverage changes from street to street.

Prefer fiber when available. Better for uploads and consistency.

Match speed to your household. 300–500 Mbps is enough for most homes.
