Internet Providers in San Antonio, TX

San Antonio has a healthy mix of cable, fiber and wireless choices, from Downtown and the Riverwalk to the fast-growing Northwest and far North Side. With several large military installations nearby, plenty of residents also need flexible plans that survive a move. Here is how the main providers compare.

Last reviewed: October 2026. Plans, prices and availability change often. Confirm with the provider for your address.

Internet by area of San Antonio

Downtown and the Riverwalk

Apartments, lofts and condos mean your building often decides what is wired in. Ask management which providers are already installed.

Alamo Heights and Olmos Park

Established neighborhoods with older homes and a mix of cable and fiber. Check Spectrum and AT&T Fiber side by side for your street.

Stone Oak and the far North Side

Newer subdivisions often have both fiber and cable. Some communities have HOA arrangements, so ask what yours includes.

Northwest Side and Helotes

Fast-growing areas where fiber coverage is expanding. Check your exact address, since new builds can differ from older streets nearby.

South and West Side

Spectrum is widely available, with fiber reaching many neighborhoods. Discounted plans for eligible households are worth checking here.

Military families and relocations

San Antonio is home to major military installations, and many families move every few years. Before you commit, ask each provider about military discounts, relocation or transfer options and early-termination fees. A no-contract 5G home plan is often the easiest to start and stop, and you can usually take the gateway with you. If you live on base, ask housing which providers are approved.

Heat, hail and flash floods

San Antonio summers are hot, and severe storms can bring hail and flash flooding. Keep your router in a cool indoor spot with airflow, and consider a battery backup for your modem and router. If you work from home, a cheap 5G backup plan or phone hotspot can keep you online during an outage.

Low-income internet plans in San Antonio

The federal Affordable Connectivity Program has ended, but providers still offer discounted plans. Look at AT&T Access and Spectrum Internet Assist for qualifying households. Eligibility often depends on programs like SNAP or Medicaid. Prices and rules change, so confirm on each provider's site.

How to choose the right San Antonio plan

Check your exact address. Fiber and cable coverage changes from one street to the next.

Match speed to your household. 300 to 500 Mbps suits most homes. Several remote workers or heavy streamers may want 1 Gig.

Prefer fiber if uploads matter. Symmetrical speeds help with video calls and large uploads.

Read the price details. Compare the promo price, how long it lasts, equipment fees and the regular rate afterward.

Think about your next move. If you may relocate soon, favor flexible or no-contract plans.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best internet provider in San Antonio?

AT&T Fiber or Google Fiber is usually best where available because of symmetrical speeds. Spectrum is the most widely available option across the city.

Is Google Fiber available in San Antonio?

Google Fiber serves parts of San Antonio. Coverage is limited to built-out neighborhoods, so check your exact address.

What internet is best for military families moving to San Antonio?

Families who may relocate often prefer no-contract 5G home internet or a plan with flexible cancellation. Ask any provider about military discounts and relocation terms.

Are there low-income internet plans in San Antonio?

Yes. AT&T Access and Spectrum Internet Assist offer discounted plans to qualifying households. Eligibility rules apply.
