Rural South Carolina internet

Rural South Carolina often relies on fixed wireless, 5G home internet or satellite. Local providers also serve some communities.

How to choose the right plan in South Carolina

Check your exact address. Coverage changes from street to street.

Prefer fiber when available. Better for uploads and consistency.

Match speed to your household. 300–500 Mbps is enough for most homes.
