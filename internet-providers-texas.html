Rural Texas internet

West Texas, Panhandle and rural counties often have limited wired service. Check fixed wireless, 5G home and satellite.

Hurricane, tornado and heat

Gulf storms, tornadoes and extreme heat can cause outages. Keep backup power ready.

Low-income internet plans in Texas

The federal Affordable Connectivity Program has ended. Look at Spectrum Internet Assist, Xfinity Internet Essentials, AT&T Access and federal Lifeline.
