Rural Vermont internet

Large parts of Vermont have limited wired broadband. Fixed wireless, 5G home internet and Starlink are the most common solutions outside the main towns.

How to choose the right plan in Vermont

Check your exact address. Coverage changes from street to street.

Prefer fiber when available. Better for uploads and consistency.

Match speed to your household. 300–500 Mbps is enough for most homes.
