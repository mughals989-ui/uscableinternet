Compare internet options across Washington

Internet choices vary between the Seattle metro, other cities, island communities, mountain towns and eastern Washington. Compare fiber for symmetrical uploads and video calls, cable for fast downloads where available, 5G home internet when coverage and eligibility fit your address, and fixed wireless or satellite in less connected areas.

Before choosing, compare the regular monthly price after promotions, equipment costs, upload speeds, data policies and cancellation terms. A provider may serve part of a city without covering every neighborhood, so check the exact service address directly with the provider.

Explore our guides to fiber internet, home internet plans and 5G home internet, or use the address checker to continue comparing options. Confirm final availability and plan terms with the provider.

Rural Washington internet

Mountain, island and eastern Washington towns often have one wired provider or none. Check fixed wireless and 5G home, then satellite.

Winter storms and wildfire smoke season

Windstorms, snow and wildfires can cause outages. A battery backup for the modem and router and a phone hotspot keep you connected during short outages.

Low-income internet plans in Washington

The federal Affordable Connectivity Program has ended, but providers still offer discounted plans. Look at Xfinity Internet Essentials and the federal Lifeline program. Ask your provider about current low-income plans. Eligibility often depends on programs like SNAP, Medicaid or SSI. Confirm current rules on the provider's website.

How to choose the right plan in Washington

Check your exact address. Coverage changes from street to street, even in one city.

Pick speed for your household. 300 to 500 Mbps suits most homes. Several remote workers or heavy gaming and streaming may need 1 Gig.

Prefer fiber when you can get it. It is the most consistent option for uploads and video calls.

Read the price details. Compare the promo price, how long it lasts, equipment fees and the regular rate after.
