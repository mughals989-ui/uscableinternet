What to verify before choosing an internet plan in West Virginia

Provider names on a state guide are a starting point, not confirmation that service reaches every street, apartment or rural address in West Virginia. Compare plans using the complete service address and the provider's current offer.

  • Confirm the exact address. Check the house number, apartment or unit, and ZIP code with the provider; nearby homes can have different networks.
  • Compare the real monthly cost. Check how long the promotional rate lasts, the regular rate afterward, equipment rental, installation, taxes, fees and any AutoPay or bundle requirements.
  • Compare performance details. Review upload as well as download speed, data limits, latency where relevant, contract terms and cancellation conditions.
  • Check building access. Renters and condo residents should ask whether the building is wired for the provider and whether installation needs approval.

The FCC National Broadband Map is useful for researching reported coverage, but it is not an order confirmation. Frequently asked questions about internet in West Virginia

Which internet providers are available in West Virginia?

Options differ by street and building. Start with the providers listed in this West Virginia guide, then enter the complete service address on each provider's official website. Communities such as Charleston, Huntington, Morgantown, Parkersburg and rural communities can have different network footprints, and a provider's statewide presence does not mean every address is serviceable.

Is fiber internet available throughout West Virginia?

No single statewide coverage statement can confirm fiber at an individual property. Fiber availability is highly address-specific across the Kanawha Valley, northern panhandle, Ohio River communities and Appalachian hollows. Check the exact house number and unit with each provider, and compare cable or fixed wireless if fiber is not offered.

What internet is best for rural homes in West Virginia?

The best option depends on the networks reaching the property. Compare any available wired service first, then check fixed wireless and satellite where cable or fiber is unavailable. For remote locations, ask about installation, equipment costs, data policies, latency and weather considerations before ordering.

How can I compare the real monthly cost?

Compare the promotional price with the regular rate after the offer ends. Include equipment, installation, taxes, fees, required bundles or AutoPay, data limits and contract terms. Confirm the final quote directly with the provider because offers can change by address and order date.
