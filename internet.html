How to choose

  • Fiber for the fastest speeds and equal upload speeds.
  • Cable for wide availability and low starting prices.
  • 5G home for no-wire setup and a trial period.
  • Satellite when no wired service reaches your address.

Frequently asked questions

Which internet type is fastest?

Fiber offers the fastest speeds in this comparison, up to 1,000 Mbps on AT&T and 940 Mbps on Verizon Fios, with equal upload speeds.

Is cable or fiber better for home internet?

Fiber has faster uploads and lower latency. Cable is widely available and often cheaper to start. Check both at your address.

Is 5G home internet a good option?

T-Mobile 5G Home starts at $35 per month with a 15-day test drive and self-install. Speeds are 72-245 Mbps and depend on your signal.

What if I live in a rural area?

Satellite internet from HughesNet and Starlink is available coast to coast. Starlink lists 50-220 Mbps.
