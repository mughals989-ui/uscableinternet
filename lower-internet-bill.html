How to Lower Your Internet Bill Without Losing Speed

Promotional rates expire. Before you renew or switch, compare the regular price, equipment fees, and whether a rival offer at your address is better.

  • Ask about current retention or loyalty offers
  • Compare the price after the promo period
  • Check if you can drop unused TV or phone lines
  • Use AutoPay/paperless discounts when they lower the real rate
