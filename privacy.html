Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information US Cable Internet collects, how we use it and your choices.

Information you give us

Address checker. The address or ZIP code you enter is used in your browser to list providers. To find your area and suggest addresses, it is sent to OpenStreetMap services (Nominatim and Photon). We do not receive or store it on our servers.

Contact form, email and phone. If you write or call us, we use your name, email, phone number and message to reply and to help with your request. If you ask us to help you order service, we may share the details you give us with the provider or sales partner handling your order.

Information collected automatically

We use Google Analytics to understand how people use the site. It uses cookies and similar technology to collect pages viewed, device and browser type, approximate location and how you arrived at the site. You can block analytics cookies in your browser or use Google's opt-out tools.

Affiliate and provider links

When you click a link to a provider, you leave our site. The provider and any affiliate network may use their own cookies and tracking. Their privacy policies apply to what they collect.

How we use information

To run and improve the site, reply to you, help with orders you ask for, prevent abuse and meet legal duties.

Do not sell or share my information

We do not sell your personal information for money. Affiliate and analytics tools may count as "sharing" under some state laws. To opt out or to make any privacy request, email info@uscableinternet.com and we will respond within the time your state's law requires.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to know what we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, and opt out of sharing. We do not discriminate against you for using these rights.

Children

This site is not for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Security and retention

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes above and use reasonable measures to protect it. No online service is completely secure.

Changes

We will post updates here and change the date below.

Accessibility

We aim to make this site usable for everyone. Report problems to info@uscableinternet.com.
