Satellite Internet: Plans, Speeds and Costs

Satellite internet can serve rural homes where fiber, cable and fixed wireless are unavailable. Compare hardware cost, monthly price, latency and data policies before you order.

What to compare

  • Equipment and installation fees
  • Typical download and upload speeds
  • Latency for video calls and gaming
  • Data thresholds or deprioritization rules
