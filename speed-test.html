UNDERSTANDING YOUR RESULTS

What do your speed test results mean?

Three simple measurements help you understand your home internet performance.

↓

Download speed

How quickly your connection receives data. Higher download speeds help with 4K streaming, gaming updates and using several devices at once.

↑

Upload speed

How quickly you send data. It matters for video meetings, sending large files, cloud backups and livestreaming.

◷

Ping (latency)

How long a signal takes to make a round trip. Lower ping generally feels more responsive for online games and video calls.

✦

Tips for a more accurate test

Close bandwidth-heavy apps, pause downloads, disconnect unused devices and run the test more than once. Wi-Fi and network congestion can affect results.

Frequently asked questions

Is this internet speed test free?

Yes. You can run the test without creating an account. Your browser downloads and uploads test data to estimate connection speed.

Why is my speed lower than the speed in my internet plan?

Wi-Fi signal strength, router performance, other devices, network congestion and the test device can all affect measured speeds.

What is a good internet speed?

For everyday browsing and HD streaming, 25–100 Mbps may be enough for a household. Larger households, 4K streaming and heavy downloads may benefit from faster plans.
