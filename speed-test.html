UNDERSTANDING YOUR RESULTS

What do your speed test results mean?

Three simple measurements help you understand your home internet performance.

↓

Download speed

How quickly your connection receives data. Higher download speeds help with 4K streaming, gaming updates and using several devices at once.

↑

Upload speed

How quickly you send data. It matters for video meetings, sending large files, cloud backups and livestreaming.

◷

Ping (latency)

How long a signal takes to make a round trip. Lower ping generally feels more responsive for online games and video calls.

✦

Tips for a more accurate test

Close bandwidth-heavy apps, pause downloads, disconnect unused devices and run the test more than once. Wi-Fi and network congestion can affect results.

Frequently asked questions

Is this internet speed test free?

Yes. You can run the test without creating an account. Your browser downloads and uploads test data to estimate connection speed.

Why is my speed lower than the speed in my internet plan?

Wi-Fi signal strength, router performance, other devices, network congestion and the test device can all affect measured speeds.

What is a good internet speed?

There is no single speed that suits every household. Consider how many people and devices are online at once, the activities they use, and whether your measured results are close to the speeds in your plan.

Get a more reliable result

For the clearest reading, connect your computer to the router with Ethernet if possible. If you use Wi-Fi, test near the router and repeat the test at different times of day. Pause downloads, cloud backups, video streams and other heavy network activity first.

Wi-Fi interference, walls, router age, device limits and network congestion can all affect results. If speeds are consistently lower than expected, restart your modem and router, test a second device, and contact your provider with the results and test times.

What to do after your test

Compare your plan's advertised download and upload speeds. Check the regular monthly price, promotional end date, equipment fees and any data policy before changing plans. You can compare home internet plans, check providers at your address, or read our guide to choosing the right internet speed.
