Frequently asked questions about internet providers by state

Does a provider listed on a state page serve every address in that state?

No. State guides are research starting points, not guarantees of service. Provider networks vary by street, building and rural area, so confirm the complete address with each provider.

Why do internet options differ between nearby cities?

Network investment, local infrastructure, building wiring and provider service territories differ by community. A provider may serve one suburb or neighborhood without reaching the next one.

How should I use the state and city guides?

Start with the state page to understand common connection types and providers, then open a city guide where available. Finish by checking your full street address with the provider.

What should I compare before choosing a provider?

Compare the regular price after promotions, download and upload speeds, equipment and installation fees, data policies, contract terms and any discount conditions.
