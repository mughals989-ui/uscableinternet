Streaming TV vs Cable TV: What to Compare

Editorial guide · Updated November 18, 2026

Live TV streaming and traditional cable both deliver live channels, but costs, equipment and internet requirements differ. Compare channel lists, total monthly price and reliability for your household.

Key differences

  • Streaming needs a solid internet connection
  • Cable may bundle internet and TV
  • Channel lineups and regional sports vary by service
  • Equipment rental can change the true monthly cost

Compare TV packages · Channel lineups

Compare the complete channel lineup

List the channels your household actually watches, including local stations, news, sports, and regional networks. A service's total channel count does not guarantee that it carries the specific channels you want. Check regional sports availability by ZIP code or service address.

Include internet and equipment costs

Live TV streaming requires broadband, so include the internet bill if you do not already pay for it. Cable packages may include equipment rental, broadcast or regional sports fees, and taxes. Streaming services may require a compatible device and several separate subscriptions.

Consider reliability and flexibility

Streaming depends on your internet connection and home Wi-Fi. Cable TV may use a provider-managed delivery network and may offer different installation and equipment arrangements. Compare contract length, cancellation rules, DVR features, simultaneous streams, and supported devices.

A practical comparison checklist

  • Confirm the exact local and sports channel lineup.
  • Add all monthly subscriptions, internet, equipment, and fees.
  • Check DVR limits, simultaneous viewing, and device support.
  • Review introductory pricing, regular rates, and cancellation terms.

Frequently asked questions

Is streaming TV cheaper than cable TV?

It depends on channel needs, subscriptions, equipment, taxes, and whether internet service is already included. Add all services before comparing.

Does live TV streaming require home internet?

Yes. Live TV streaming uses an internet connection, and quality depends on available bandwidth, network stability, and the number of simultaneous streams.

Does cable TV include local channels and sports?

Channel availability varies by provider, package, and location. Check the exact lineup, regional sports fees, and local channel coverage before ordering.
