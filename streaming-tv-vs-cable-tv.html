Streaming TV vs Cable TV: What to Compare

Live TV streaming and traditional cable both deliver live channels, but costs, equipment and internet requirements differ. Compare channel lists, total monthly price and reliability for your household.

Key differences

  • Streaming needs a solid internet connection
  • Cable may bundle internet and TV
  • Channel lineups and regional sports vary by service
  • Equipment rental can change the true monthly cost
