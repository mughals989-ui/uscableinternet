T-Mobile 5G Home Internet: Plans, Speeds & Eligibility

T-Mobile Home Internet uses the cellular network and a home gateway rather than a cable or fiber line. Availability is checked by address and may be limited by local tower capacity, even where mobile 5G coverage exists.

Who may want to compare T-Mobile 5G Home Internet?

Homes that qualify for the service and want simple self-installation, especially where wired broadband choices are limited.

Check your addressVisit T-Mobile 5G Home Internet official site

What to verify before ordering

  1. Check eligibility using the exact service address; mobile 5G coverage alone does not guarantee home internet sign-up availability.
  2. Review the typical speed range for the specific plan and understand that signal strength and tower congestion affect performance.
  3. Compare the standalone monthly price with any eligible voice-line discount and check AutoPay conditions.
  4. Confirm gateway, shipping, trial, return and cancellation terms before replacing your existing service.
  5. Test the connection in the rooms where you use it most and compare evening performance before cancelling a wired plan.
