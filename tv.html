Cable TV, satellite TV or live streaming: which fits?

Choose based on the channels you actually watch, local sports availability, internet reliability and the total price after introductory offers.

TV optionGood fit forBefore you subscribe
Cable TVHouseholds that want a traditional channel lineup and may bundle TV with home internet.Check local channel packages, broadcast or regional sports fees, equipment charges and bundle price changes.
Satellite TVHomes outside cable footprints and viewers who want broad national channel packages.Check dish installation, line-of-sight requirements, contract terms, equipment and weather-related interruptions.
Live TV streamingViewers who prefer app-based service, supported devices and no satellite dish.Check local broadcast stations by ZIP code, sports and regional sports networks, simultaneous streams, DVR limits and internet data use.

Compare the channels—not just the monthly price

Before choosing a package, make a short list of must-have channels: local ABC, CBS, FOX and NBC stations, national sports networks, regional sports networks, news, kids' channels and premium movie services. Then compare that list against the provider's lineup for your ZIP code. Channel names and availability can vary by market, and streaming services may change packages without much notice.

  • Confirm local stations and regional sports coverage for your ZIP code.
  • Add equipment, broadcast, regional sports and other recurring fees to the advertised price.
  • Check the price after any introductory period and whether a contract applies.
  • For streaming, confirm device compatibility, internet speed, simultaneous streams and DVR rules.
  • Compare the cost of a TV package with the streaming apps you already pay for to avoid duplicate subscriptions.
