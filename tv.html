Frequently asked questions

Which TV plan has no cable box fees?

Spectrum TV Select Plus lists no cable box fees, at $69.99 per month for 12 months.

How much does DIRECTV streaming cost?

DIRECTV advertises $59.99 per month for the first month. The price rises after the promotion, and fees and taxes apply. Check current terms on the DIRECTV site.

Is there a price guarantee?

DISH Top 120 lists a 2-year price guarantee at $84.99 per month with 190 channels.

What is the difference between live TV streaming and streaming apps?

Live TV streaming services such as YouTube TV, Sling TV, Fubo, Hulu + Live TV, Philo, Frndly TV and DIRECTV stream live channels over the internet. Peacock and Paramount+ are on-demand apps with some live sports.

Do I need internet to stream TV?

Yes. Streaming TV needs a home internet connection. Compare internet plans on our internet page.

Do prices change after the first year?

Some promotional rates end after a set period. Read the terms on the provider site before you order.
