Verizon Home Internet: Fios & 5G Plans Compared

Verizon sells home internet over two different technologies: Fios fiber in parts of the Northeast and Mid-Atlantic, and 5G fixed wireless in selected coverage areas. They are not interchangeable; check which service is actually offered at your address.

Who may want to compare Verizon Home Internet?

Households in Fios areas that want a wired fiber connection, or eligible homes with a strong 5G signal that prefer a wireless self-install option.

What to verify before ordering

  1. Identify whether the offer is Verizon Fios or Verizon 5G Home; compare the technology, not just the brand.
  2. For Fios, verify the speed tier, upload speed, router terms and installation details.
  3. For 5G Home, confirm address eligibility, expected speed range, signal placement and any capacity limits.
  4. Check whether a mobile-plan bundle or AutoPay is required for the advertised discount.
  5. Compare the standard price, equipment terms, trial/cancellation rules and final checkout offer.

Verizon Fios and Verizon 5G Home are different services

Verizon Home Internet can refer to Fios fiber or 5G fixed wireless. These use different networks and have different eligibility, performance and installation details. Confirm which technology is being offered at the exact address before comparing a monthly price.

For Fios, compare the wired speed tier, upload speed, router terms and installation requirements. For 5G Home, check the expected speed range, gateway placement, signal conditions and whether local network capacity affects eligibility. A mobile 5G signal on a phone does not guarantee that a home internet plan is available.

Common questions

Is Verizon Fios the same as Verizon 5G Home?

No. Fios is a wired fiber service; 5G Home uses a cellular connection and home gateway.

Why does Verizon show a different price for another address?

Availability, plan type, speed tier and eligible mobile or AutoPay discounts can vary by address and account.

Compare the actual offer at your home with other internet plans, review fiber options and check availability at your address before choosing.
