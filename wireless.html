Frequently asked questions

What is 5G home internet?

It delivers home internet over a cellular 5G network. T-Mobile 5G Home starts at $35 per month with a voice line and self-install.

Which unlimited plan has the biggest hotspot?

AT&T Unlimited Premium lists 60 GB of hotspot data, T-Mobile Go5G Plus lists 50 GB and Verizon Unlimited Plus lists 30 GB.

Are the prices per line?

Yes. The unlimited plan prices shown are per line and some need AutoPay.
