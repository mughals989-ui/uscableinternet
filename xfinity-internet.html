What to verify before ordering

  1. Confirm the exact plan and speed tier available at your street address; plans differ by market.
  2. Check whether the advertised price requires AutoPay, paperless billing or new-customer eligibility.
  3. Compare the promotional price with the regular rate and note the length of the promotion.
  4. Review modem/router charges, installation, activation, data policies and any optional add-ons.
  5. Read the broadband facts label and verify the full checkout total before placing an order.

Xfinity cable internet: what to compare

Xfinity offers different speed tiers and promotions by market. The plan displayed for one ZIP code may not be available at another address nearby. Use the full street address to identify the eligible plan and review its broadband facts label before ordering.

Compare the promotional rate with the regular monthly rate, and check which discounts require AutoPay, paperless billing or new-customer eligibility. Include modem or gateway charges, installation, taxes, optional add-ons and any data policy in your total-cost comparison.

Common questions

Does Xfinity offer the same plans everywhere?

No. Plan names, speed tiers, pricing and promotional conditions vary by market and service address.

Is Xfinity cable the same as fiber?

Not necessarily. Check the technology named for the specific offer. Compare upload speed and latency alongside download speed when evaluating cable against fiber.

Compare the actual offer at your home with other internet plans, review fiber options and check availability at your address before choosing.
